# Tiers for arXiv 2608.30041 (SkillGuard)

Anchor: [2608.30041](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30041), "Reachability-Based Capability Confinement for LLM Agents under Indirect Prompt Injection". Notes: `notes/skillguard_notes.md`. Search space: `2608.30041_search_space.md` (238 papers). Built 2026-09-19.

Direction 1 (cited, 30) and direction 2 (citing, 0 — skipped) are kept in full. From the 208 sweep-only papers, 25 are kept against the cap the user set; the remaining 183 are listed in the cut table at the end. Two papers (2403.14720, 2306.05499) arrived under both D1 and D3 and are kept as cited papers.

The notes name four things, and "closest note" below refers to one of them:
- **SIG** — the Skill Impact Graph: nodes are security-relevant states, skill invocations induce transitions.
- **Reachability/min-cut** — reachability from current state q to forbidden set B, unsafe paths disconnected at minimal cost.
- **Steerability signatures** — explicit type and permitted value range per skill parameter, rather than judging the natural-language semantics of an argument.
- **Reference monitor** — inline mediation of every skill invocation.

## Where the space is crowded and where it is thin

**Crowded.** Runtime mediation of tool calls is dense: Progent, CaMeL, ClawGuard, AgentVisor, MiniScope, Prismata, MTGuard and AttriGuard all intercept invocations and check them against a policy, and several already claim near-zero ASR on AgentDojo. Policy-as-rules-over-tool-arguments (the steerability-signature idea) is essentially occupied by Progent. Attack and benchmark papers are the densest region of the whole space — of the 183 cut papers, a large share are IPI red-teaming and benchmark variants.

**Thin.** Three gaps:
1. *Graphs of skill composition.* AgentArmor builds a graph from a trace after the fact; nothing in the space builds a graph over *declared skill semantics* before execution, and nothing computes reachability to a forbidden set over it. The SIG has no direct competitor here.
2. *Minimum-cut as the restriction rule.* KubeCap and MiniScope minimise privilege, but by static inference and hierarchy reconstruction, not by an optimal cut with an explicit cost. No paper in the space poses capability restriction as an optimisation problem with a stated objective.
3. *Guarantees.* Only Prismata (bounded mislabelling) and Governed Individuation (proved non-widening of authority) state a formal property; 2605.16976 says so explicitly for the field as a whole. If SkillGuard proves a reachability property, that is where the space is emptiest.

Also thin: contamination *detection* is assumed rather than solved by the confinement papers, and only NeuroTaint and DASGuard address provenance in agent settings — SkillGuard's "when a contamination event is committed" depends on a component nobody in Tier 1 supplies.

## Caveats

- Based on abstracts only. No full text was read; claims here trace to the abstract dump at `scratch/abstracts.md`, one abstract per paper for all 238 papers in the search space.
- Direction 2 (citing the anchor) is empty: the anchor was posted 2026-08-30, 20 days before the sweep, so the search space skipped it under the 90-day rule. Nothing in these tiers reacts to SkillGuard.
- Selection of the 25 sweep papers is a reading judgement against the notes, not a score. Sweep papers with strong index-term overlap but no bearing on the notes (capability architectures for memory safety, fault injection, watermarking, privacy attacks on agent memory) are in the cut table.
- 8 references of the anchor were unresolved by `resolve_references` and never entered the search space; they are listed in `2608.30041_search_space.md` and are not tiered here.
